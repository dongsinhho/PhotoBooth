# Competitor Research Snapshot

**Checked:** 2026-10-25  
**Status:** Desk research only. Product claims and prices are sourced from public vendor pages and have not been independently tested. Recheck before using in public marketing or a purchasing decision.

## Summary

Existing products already cover camera control, templates/frames, cloud photo delivery, remote management, and QR-based guest flows. The opportunity should not be defined as “no one has these features.” Test whether a free-to-use commercial app that reuses operator-owned hardware, allows self-service installation, supports flexible local/LAN/cloud delivery, and charges only for optional support solves a meaningful unmet need.

## What this research does and does not prove

### Supported by the cited public pages
- Hardware + software packages in the Vietnamese market can be expensive; the listed MayPhotoBooth configurations start at 99M VND on the cited page.
- VietBooth publicly claims Windows and several camera-vendor integrations plus cloud storage.
- Lumilab publicly describes multi-tenant web management and Android/Linux player variants.
- WervieBooth publicly markets QR payment, remote management, and template creation.
- A foreign standalone software subscription model exists.

### Not proven
- That customers consider software licensing their biggest pain.
- That a large enough segment wants to self-install or self-support.
- That target cameras and printers are practical to support on Linux.
- That no competitor offers free, local-first, or configurable software.
- That customers will pay for support after receiving the software at no charge.
- That the listed vendors' feature claims work on every model or under real high-volume use.

## Positioning hypotheses to test

1. **Reuse existing hardware:** operators perceive current bundled setup costs as a barrier and can supply their own PC/camera/printer.
2. **Free commercial use:** a zero software usage fee changes the evaluation decision even if setup remains self-service.
3. **Self-service configuration:** operators value changing countdowns, shot count, delays, frames, and output behavior without vendor intervention.
4. **Optional support:** a segment can install independently but will purchase help for difficult hardware or outages.
5. **Local/LAN-first:** some operators prefer avoiding mandatory cloud storage and recurring cloud bills.
6. **Legacy-device support:** older equipment can provide reliable required capture/output capabilities on a supported OS.

Interview notes should score these hypotheses separately and include counter-evidence. Do not turn this hypothesis list into marketing claims before validation.
